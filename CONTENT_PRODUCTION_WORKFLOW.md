# Production Content Workflow

You are not allowed to generate filler content simply to complete a page.

For every article:

1. Read `SUMAN_EDITORIAL_CONTEXT.md`.
2. Read the relevant project data.
3. Determine the article's search intent.
4. Determine which parts are supported by actual project information.
5. Draft approximately 700–850 words.
6. Include concrete examples.
7. Include Suman's actual perspective where supported.
8. Explain technical decisions in terms of product outcomes.
9. Add SEO metadata.
10. Add internal links.
11. Add related articles.
12. Add FAQ only if genuinely useful.
13. Calculate word count.
14. Remove repetition.
15. Remove generic AI phrases.
16. Verify technical claims.
17. Ensure no unsupported metrics exist.
18. Ensure the article does not sound like marketing copy.
19. Ensure the final article is useful even to someone who does not know Suman.
20. Store the result in the data layer.

## NEVER

- fabricate experiences
- fabricate metrics
- fabricate customers
- fabricate technology
- fabricate architecture
- fabricate performance
- fabricate business outcomes
- invent quotes
- use generic motivational introductions
- keyword stuff
- pad word count
- repeat the same idea across multiple paragraphs
- overuse "think/thinking"
- turn every article into a HamroLink advertisement
- write every article in the same structure
- make every article sound like an AI thought-leadership post

## FINAL TEST

Read the article once as:
1. a developer
2. a founder
3. a Google search visitor

It should work for all three.

Then ask:
"Does this sound like Suman, or does this sound like an AI writing about Suman?"
If it sounds like the second, rewrite it.
